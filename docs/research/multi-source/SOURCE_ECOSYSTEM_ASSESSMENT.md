- **Authentication:** Brabble documents an API key while its FAQ disagrees; GitHub offers public and authenticated REST access; Devfolio's organizer export needs organizer access. Authentication for the other proposed bulk paths is unverified. Do not treat absence of a login on a page as an API permission.
- **Commercial reuse and extraction:** Devpost's published terms explicitly bar crawling and scraping. For the other platforms, this assessment found no blanket Hacktor commercial ingestion grant. A platform-specific agreement must define allowed fields, attribution, retention, deletion, and onward display.
- **Rate, pagination, and caching:** Brabble and GitHub publish controls. For the other sources, these are unknown until an approved feed or export is documented. Any future connector needs bounded pagination, conditional fetch or cache rules, backoff, and a kill switch.
- **Freshness and geography:** Brabble advertises an hourly open-listing snapshot and city filters for 12 Indian cities; city filtering excludes online events. Other platform pages provide examples, not measured geographic completeness. Historical galleries may retain projects while event rules and award labels can change.
- **Historical results:** ETHGlobal, lablab.ai, Devfolio, Devpost, DoraHacks, and TAIKAI visibly expose projects or galleries. Award status must be tied to a specific platform or organizer record. GitHub and HackRep cannot independently verify submission or prize status.
- **Additional sources:** No additional connector is recommended yet. An independently licensed organizer consortium or open event feed could add authoritative rules and geography missing from these platforms, subject to the same permission review.
