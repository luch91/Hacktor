# Visual direction at Gate 1

Hacktor uses a near-black canvas, precise violet interaction cues, and a self-authored orbital relationship drawing. The hero pairs a compact statement and action with the drawing, then reveals the product method and two distinct entry paths. Its geometry is original SVG, with a slow orbital emphasis that stops under reduced-motion preferences.

Studio uses the stored session title, confirmed context, hypotheses, immutable idea versions, and actual lineage edges. Its center has priority. The left rail gives context and stage progression; the right inspector exposes the selected version and existing actions. Constellation and List are views over the same idea array, with no synthetic graph links. Two-parent relationships are visible on the child and in the lineage record.

Tokens remain in `packages/ui/src/tokens.css`: dark `#050505`, `#0A0A0A`, `#111111`, `#F6F4F8`, `#C386FF`, `#D5A9FF`, `#262228`; light `#F8F7F4` with dark text and deeper violet. Syne, Inter and IBM Plex Mono are locally packaged font files.

The XDesign reference contributes editorial pacing and a focal visual. Cattleya contributes navigational clarity and structured content framing. The product does not use their licensed imagery or exact composition. Missing Hacktor HTML previews and design-system document prevent pixel-level comparison; this direction remains proposed until rendered evidence and owner review.
